What is Moodlecat?

Moodlecat is a playful and emotional drawing app where you create a small cat doodle every day to capture your mood. Each cat is sealed with a time capsule: you can unlock it in a week, a month, or even a year.

While your cat sleeps, others can see it — and gently respond with a few emojis or short taglines. You can’t read your own cat until it wakes.

Core Features

  • ✨ Daily cat doodles to express your mood
  • ⏳ Lock your drawing for 7, 30, or 365 days
  • 🌌 Let your cats "sleep" in a personal star map
  • 👀 View and gently tag others' sleeping cats with emoji + 3-letter codes
  • 🪐 Unlock your cat in the future and rediscover how you felt

A Gentle Community

Moodlecat isn't about likes or followers. It's about quiet presence, shared moments, and anonymous kindness. Our world is filled with sleeping cats — each one holding someone's private moment.
