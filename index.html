Visual Memory of Everyday Life

日常生活的视觉记忆

Moodlecat began with a very small idea: draw a cat that captures how you feel today, seal it inside a time capsule, and meet it again in a week, a month, or a year.

Moodlecat 最初来自一个很小的想法：画一只代表今天心情的猫， 把它封进时间胶囊，在一周、一个月或一年之后重新遇见它。

But everyday memories are more than moods. A strange conversation, a tiny accident, a joke, or an ordinary day can all become something worth remembering.

但日常记忆并不只有情绪。它也可能是一句奇怪的对话、一个小意外、 一个笑话，或者某个后来突然觉得值得留下来的普通日子。

Moodlecat is growing into a visual memory project: a place for turning fleeting moments into doodles, expressions, comics and visual stories. Draw → Save → Rediscover.

Moodlecat 正在逐渐成为一个视觉记忆项目：把容易消失的瞬间转化成 涂鸦、表情、漫画和视觉故事。画下来 → 保存 → 再次遇见。

Products

产品

Moodlecat is developing two connected products for turning everyday life into visual memories.

Moodlecat 正在开发两个相互连接的产品， 把日常生活转化成可以再次遇见的视觉记忆。

SimpleComic

SimpleComic / 简单漫画

MoodleCat Story

A simple drawing and comic-making app for turning everyday moments into visual stories. Built for iPad, it moves from expressions and stickers to single-panel comics and longer sequences.

一个把日常瞬间画成视觉故事的简单漫画创作 App。 它主要面向 iPad，从简单表情、贴纸和单格漫画，到更完整的连续漫画， 都可以在这里慢慢完成。

We are building it around the way we actually draw: with Apple Pencil, simple storytelling tools, and as little friction as possible between remembering something and drawing it down. Ordinary life deserves a visual form.

我们按照自己真正每天画画的方式设计它：Apple Pencil、简单的故事工具， 以及尽可能缩短“突然想起一件事”和“真正把它画下来”之间的距离。 普通生活也值得拥有一种可以被留下来的视觉形式。

Moodlecat Moments

Moodlecat Moments

Visual memory and time capsules 视觉记忆与时间胶囊

Capture a feeling before it disappears. Draw a small cat, doodle or expression, then seal it for 7 days, 30 days or 365 days. While it sleeps, the memory rests in your personal star map. When it wakes, it returns with a small piece of the person you were when you made it.

在感受消失之前，把它画下来。画一只猫、一幅小涂鸦，或者一个代表此刻的表情， 然后把它封存 7 天、30 天或 365 天。在时间胶囊沉睡期间，记忆会停留在你的个人星图里。 等它醒来时，它会带回一点当时的你。

  • ✨ Draw a mood or moment with a quick doodle
  • ✨ 用简单的涂鸦捕捉此刻的情绪或经历
  • ⏳ Lock each drawing for 7, 30, or 365 days
  • ⏳ 把每幅画封存 7 天、30 天或 365 天
  • 🌌 Let sleeping cats rest in your personal star map
  • 🌌 让睡着的猫停留在属于你的个人星图里
  • 💬 Share gently with emojis and short tags
  • 💬 用 Emoji 或短标签轻轻回应
  • 🪐 Rediscover the feeling when the capsule wakes
  • 🪐 等时间胶囊醒来，再次遇见当时的感受

Roadmap

开发计划

SimpleComic is currently being prepared for release. It is being developed as the richer iPad experience within MoodleCat Story, while Moodlecat Moments remains the lighter visual-memory and time-capsule experience. Both are evolving as we draw, test and learn from real use. Moodlecat Moments is also currently in development.

SimpleComic 目前正在准备发布。它是 MoodleCat Story 旗下、 主要面向 iPad 的完整绘画与漫画创作体验；Moodlecat Moments 则继续作为更轻量的视觉记录与时间胶囊体验。两个方向都会随着真实创作、 测试和使用反馈不断演化。Moodlecat Moments 也正在开发中。

Shanghai Bird builds software, visual experiences and cultural projects around one long-term question: how can memory become something we can see, revisit — and eventually touch? Moodlecat explores the visual side of that question.
