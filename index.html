What is Moodlecat?

Moodlecat is a playful and emotional drawing app where you create a small cat doodle to capture your mood. Each cat is sealed inside a time capsule that can be unlocked in a week, a month, or even a year.

While your cat sleeps, others may see it and respond gently with a few emojis or short tags. You cannot read your own cat again until it wakes.

Moodlecat is designed as a quiet space for relaxing doodles, private reflection, and rediscovering moments that might otherwise be forgotten.

Core Features

  • ✨ Create relaxing daily cat doodles
  • ⏳ Lock each drawing for 7, 30, or 365 days
  • 🌌 Let sleeping cats rest in your personal star map
  • 👀 View sleeping cats shared by other people
  • 💬 Respond gently with emojis and short tags
  • 🪐 Unlock your cat later and rediscover how you felt

A Gentle Community

Moodlecat is not built around likes, follower counts, rankings, or public popularity.

It is designed around quiet presence, shared moments, and small anonymous responses. Its world is filled with sleeping cats, each holding someone's private moment until it is ready to wake.
