Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

HUYIJI CULTURAL AND CREATIVE (SHANGHAI) CO., LTD. ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SimpleComic (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we do not require

The App does not require registration or login. It does not require your name, email address, phone number, or other identity information. The App does not include advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, public social features, or user-to-user chat.

2. User-created content

The App allows you to create comics, stickers, text, layers, and drawings. This content is stored locally on your device, including project files and thumbnails.

The App does not automatically upload your projects, drawings, text, thumbnails, or backup files to our servers, and does not use this content for advertising or behavioral analytics.

You may export a project backup or share it through the system share sheet with another app, cloud service, or storage location. Files exported by you are managed by you and the third-party service you choose.

3. Photo Library access

When you explicitly choose Save to Photos, the App requests permission to add the exported PNG image to your photo library. The App does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos.

4. Local Network and Bluetooth access

The App's iPhone companion feature uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to discover and connect an iPhone and iPad on the same local network.

Local Network and Bluetooth access are used only for device discovery, pairing, and companion communication. The App does not use these permissions for location tracking, advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics.

Device names and connection status are used only during the current connection session. They are not uploaded to a server and connection history is not retained.

5. Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs, or other third-party services intended to collect user behavior.

The App uses Apple system frameworks, including Photos, MultipeerConnectivity, and system file-sharing features. Apple system frameworks may process necessary system-level information under Apple's privacy policies.

6. Deleting data

You can delete projects from the project list. Deleting the App removes projects, thumbnails, and related settings stored in the App's local storage. Backup files exported to Files, a cloud service, or another app must be deleted separately from that location.

7. Data retention

Project data is generally retained until you delete the project, delete the App, or overwrite the related data. The App does not store project data on our servers.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose creative tool. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information, and the App does not require users to submit identity information.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If the App's data practices change, we will update this page and the Last updated date.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月22日

HUYIJI CULTURAL AND CREATIVE (SHANGHAI) CO., LTD.（以下简称“我们”）尊重并保护用户的隐私。本隐私政策说明 SimpleComic 简单漫画（以下简称“本 App”）如何处理信息。

一、我们不要求的用户信息

本 App 不要求用户注册或登录，也不要求提供姓名、电子邮箱、电话号码或其他身份信息。本 App 不包含广告、订阅、应用内购买、公开社交功能或用户之间的聊天功能。

二、用户创建的内容

本 App 允许用户创建漫画、贴纸、文字、图层和绘画内容。这些内容默认保存在用户设备上的本地 App 存储中，包括项目文件和项目缩略图。

本 App 不会自动将用户的项目、绘画内容、文字、缩略图或备份文件上传到我们的服务器，也不会将这些内容用于广告或用户行为分析。

用户可以主动导出项目备份，或通过系统分享功能将文件发送到用户选择的其他 App、云盘或存储位置。导出后的文件由用户和所选择的第三方服务分别负责管理。

三、照片图库权限

当用户主动选择“保存到照片”时，本 App 会请求仅用于添加照片的系统权限，并将导出的 PNG 图片写入照片图库。本 App 不读取、浏览或上传用户已有的照片。

四、本地网络和 Bluetooth 权限

本 App 的 iPhone companion 功能使用 Apple 提供的 MultipeerConnectivity 框架，用于发现并连接同一局域网内的 iPhone 和 iPad。

本地网络和 Bluetooth 权限仅用于设备发现、配对和 companion 通信。本 App 不使用这些权限进行定位、广告投放、用户追踪或行为分析。

设备名称或连接状态只在连接过程和当前会话中用于显示和配对，不会被上传到服务器，也不会保存连接历史。

五、分析、广告和第三方服务

本 App 不包含第三方广告 SDK、分析 SDK、崩溃统计 SDK 或其他用于收集用户行为的第三方服务。本 App 使用 Apple 提供的系统框架，包括 Photos、MultipeerConnectivity 和系统文件分享功能。

六、数据删除

用户可以在作品列表页删除项目。删除本 App 会删除保存在本 App 本地存储中的项目、缩略图和相关设置。用户主动导出到“文件”、云盘或其他 App 的备份文件，需要在相应位置单独删除。

七、数据保存期限

本 App 的项目数据通常会保留到用户删除项目、删除本 App，或使用新的内容覆盖相关数据为止。本 App 不在服务器上保存用户项目数据。

八、儿童隐私

本 App 是一款通用的创作工具，不主动收集儿童的个人信息，也不要求用户提交个人身份信息。

九、隐私政策变更

如果本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面的内容和“最后更新日期”。
