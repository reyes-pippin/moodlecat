MoodleCat Story

SimpleComic

A simple drawing and comic-making app for turning everyday moments into visual stories. Designed for iPad, Apple Pencil and the way ordinary life becomes worth drawing.

一个把日常瞬间画成视觉故事的简单漫画创作 App。 它主要面向 iPad 和 Apple Pencil，让那些值得留下的普通生活被画下来。

About the project

关于项目

SimpleComic is being developed as the richer creative experience within Moodlecat. It is designed to make the distance between remembering something and drawing it down as short as possible.

SimpleComic 是 Moodlecat 旗下更完整的创作体验。 它希望尽可能缩短“想起一件事”和“把它画下来”之间的距离。

Release status

发布状态

SimpleComic is currently being prepared for release. Product details, features and the final app name may continue to change during testing.

SimpleComic 目前正在准备发布。产品细节、功能和最终名称， 都可能在测试过程中继续调整。
